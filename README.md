## 💡 The Kind of Engineering I Enjoy

I’m most interested in work where there’s more to solve than a single feature — systems that combine **application development, infrastructure, data, devices, and intelligence**.

I like owning problems end to end, understanding the trade-offs behind architectural decisions, and building software that stays dependable as both the product and the team grow.

---

## 🧠 How I Think About Software

> **Good engineering should make a product easier to evolve, not harder.**

I value clear architecture, thoughtful abstractions, strong fundamentals, sensible performance work, observability, security, and maintainable code.

I also believe complexity has to earn its place. The best solution is often the simplest one that meets the real requirements and leaves room for what comes next.
